# Master volume {#master-volume}

The volume of all sound together. **100%** plays the sound as the cards
make it; up to **200%** makes quiet games louder.

The game keeps playing on this page, so you hear the change at once.

> **Ctrl+F8** turns all sound off and on while a game runs.

# Volume {#volume}

The volume of one sound source, **100%** being as loud as the card makes
it, from **0%** (silent) to **200%**. The meter at the right shows how
loud it plays now, so you can see which source a sound comes from:

- **PC speaker**: beeps, and the music of the earliest games.
- **Sound Blaster**: sound effects and speech.
- **FM synthesizer**: AdLib and Sound Blaster music.
- **Gravis Ultrasound**, **MIDI**: music.
- **AWE32 wavetable**: the AWE32's music, apart from its Sound Blaster
  sound.
- **CD audio**: music played from a game's CD.
- **Covox/Disney**, **Tandy/PCjr**: those sound devices' speech, sound
  and music.
- **Disk noise**: the hard and floppy disk sounds.

For instance, if a game's music drowns the speech, turn down **FM synthesizer** or
**MIDI**.

# PC speaker filter {#speaker-filter}

Softens the PC speaker's harsh beeps to sound like the small speaker in a
real PC. Leave it **on**, unless you like the raw square wave.

# Sound Blaster filter {#sb-filter}

**auto** filters the Sound Blaster's sound as the real card of the chosen
model did, making old low-quality samples less hissy. **off** plays them
unfiltered: crisper, but harsher.

# Reverb {#reverb}

Adds the echo of a room, from **tiny** to **huge**, to the music of the FM
synthesizer, the Ultrasound and MIDI. **medium** or **large** gives flat
FM music some depth. Sound effects stay dry.

# Chorus {#chorus}

Makes the music of the FM synthesizer, the Ultrasound and MIDI sound
fuller and wider, from **light** to **strong**.

# Dry/wet mix {#effect-mix}

How much of the effect you hear: **0%** the music alone, **50%** the music
and the effect both in full, **100%** the effect alone.
